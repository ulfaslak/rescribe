## Runtime topology

Four execution contexts, each with a distinct job:

| Context | File | Lifetime | Does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Service worker | `background.ts` | ephemeral, event-driven | toolbar click and keyboard command (`onToggle`), badge, the windows and tabs. A click while `idle` or `stopped` on a tab other than the app tab (a waiting recording never blocks a start elsewhere: `stopped` starts like `idle`) opens the **start dialog** for that tab (`openStartDialog`: `start/start.html?tab=<id>`) and the app tab in the background meanwhile; a click while the start dialog is open closes it (the button toggles it), or moves it to the clicked tab when it was for another; a dialog page that sits as a tab in a browser window ("Show as tab") is closed as a tab, never with its window (`closeDialogs`; the pages' own `ui/popup.ts` does the same). **Start Capture** arrives as `start-capture` (`startCapture`): refused while `recording` or `starting` (the app tab comes forward then: it may be waiting on the microphone prompt); the one app tab is ensured (`ensureAppTab`, one in flight at a time: closes duplicates, reloads one that does not answer, badge `!` when none answers; a new tab goes into the last focused normal window, never "the current window", which can be a dialog's), then `chrome.tabCapture.getMediaStreamId` with `consumerTabId` = the app tab (the id is bound to a render process; the app tab is cross-origin isolated and not in the service worker's process), issued last because it expires after 10 s, then, unless the dialog for that tab is gone by now (the human backed out while the app tab booted: cancelled), `toggle-recording` to that tab only, with the dialog's microphone choice; `ok` once the app tab took the message, else the answer (`StartResult`) says why not. The app tab is focused only on `focus-app` (the app tab needs the human; "Open Rescribe" in the other pages), on a click on the app tab itself, and on a click while `starting` or `recorded`. A click while `recording`, a click on the app tab itself while `stopped`, and the app tab's `ask-to-save`, open the **stop dialog** (`openStopDialog`: one popup-type window, found by URL and focused when already open). Both dialogs are popup-type windows placed near the top right of the last focused normal window, or where Chrome puts them when it refuses those bounds (`openDialogWindow`). `open-progress` (the stop dialog on a save; the start dialog's "Show") opens the **processing tab** for one recording in the last focused normal window, in front, focusing an open one. It removes no dialog window: the stop dialog closes itself once the export answers (removing it within milliseconds of Save as… crashed Chrome for Testing, ENVIRONMENT_NOTES). Exposes `globalThis.rescribe.onToggle`, `openStopDialog` and `startCapture` for DevTools and the e2e harness |
| App tab | `app/app.ts` | while open; pinned; warns on close when recording or exporting | owns the recorder, the audio graph, OPFS writes, the decision on a stopped recording (`app/decide.ts`), the transcription pipeline (`app/pipeline.ts`), export, settings UI, library UI. `query-status` answers the recorder's state: `idle` while a transcription runs, so Start Capture still starts a recording; the pill shows the transcription then, the badge does not; `starting` while a start is under way (its captures, a microphone prompt), so a second Start is refused and this tab brought forward; `stopped` while a recording waits for its decision (`refreshPending`: the newest `stopped` record; badge `SAVE`), `recorded` for the moment the recorder drains or an export runs. Answers the stop dialog's `query-pending` (the live recording, also while it drains after its stream died, else the waiting one; nothing while a decision is applied) and, before it answers anything on startup, reads what waits (`refreshPending` first, so the service worker's first `query-status` is never a stale `idle`); `decide` (`applyDecision`: stop the live recorder first, then `decide`; the outcome once the export or the deletion is done); `query-progress` (`progressInfo`: whether a decision is being applied to the recording (`decide.ts` `isDeciding`, or the live stop) and the pipeline's latest `PipelineProgress` for it, from `lastProgress`, kept per held recording in queue order and dropped at `done` or when the run rejects; without an id, whichever recording is held, with its title); `folder-changed` (the start dialog changed the Settings folder: show it). A `call-ended` from the recorded tab (toolbar path) or any tab (picker path, like speaker and roster events) sends `ask-to-save`, as does a stream that died. Sends `focus-app` (`comeForward`) when a start left in the background needs the human: the microphone permission is at `prompt` (an unknown answer counts as `prompt`), the start failed, or a `toggle-recording` arrives while a start is pending or while not idle (the service worker sent it on a stale `idle`). On the toolbar path the microphone is captured **before** the tab-capture id is consumed: consuming it mutes the captured tab for the human until the recorder plays it back, and a prompt takes as long as the human takes; the microphone is the dialog's choice (`toggle-recording` `mic`, `micDeviceId`), the Record section's checkbox and Settings' device on the picker path. A microphone that is merely unavailable is a note on the recording, not a reason to come forward. Settings changed elsewhere (the dialog's microphone choice) are shown through `chrome.storage.onChanged`, own saves skipped. **Single owner:** a second app tab that finds a sibling answering `query-status` renders only "already open in another tab" and never listens |
| Content script | `content/meet.ts` | per Meet page | observes the DOM, emits speaker events, the roster (on change, and every 30 s while tiles are on screen, so a recording started into a stable call holds one) and `call-ended` (no tiles for 5 s after there were some) to the extension via `chrome.runtime.sendMessage`; mirrors every message as a `rescribe:event` CustomEvent on `window` for the e2e harness (Meet's scripts cannot read the detail across worlds) |
| Start dialog | `start/start.ts` | while open; one at a time | opened by the toolbar click for one tab (`?tab=<id>`, read back with `chrome.tabs.get`: its title and host); the microphone `<select>` (`ui/mics.ts`, saved to Settings on change), the Settings folder with Change… (the picker inside its own click; `setExportRoot`, then `folder-changed` to the app tab), a one-liner about what the app tab holds (`query-pending`: a recording waiting to be saved, with "Open Rescribe"; `query-progress`: one being saved or transcribed, the step of how many, with "Show" → `open-progress`; polled once a second, the app tab may still be booting) and **Start Capture**: `start-capture` to the service worker, the window closes on `ok`, shows the error otherwise. Escape closes it; closing it starts nothing |
| Stop dialog | `stop/stop.ts` | while open; one at a time | a view over the owning app tab's pending recording (`query-pending` by tab id through `ui/apptab.ts`, once a second: the title, the elapsed time, `recording` or `ended`, where Save will put it) with Save, Save as… and Discard (a two-step); sends one `decide` and closes on the answer (where the video went, or why not); a save also asks the service worker for the processing tab (`open-progress`) the moment it is sent, so the tab follows the export while the window still says `Saving…`. Escape or closing the window decides nothing. Its own clicks host what a hidden app tab cannot: the folder permission prompt (`ensureExportPermission`) and the pickers (Save with no folder chosen picks the Settings folder; Save as… stores the handle under `export:<id>` for the app tab) |
| Processing tab | `progress/progress.ts` | while open; one per recording | a normal tab for one recording (`?id=`): once a second, the record from IndexedDB (`getRecording`: the truth on disk, readable with the app tab closed) and the app tab's `query-progress` for it; a headline per state (saving the video, with a bar; the pipeline's step plan through `ui/steps.ts`: its headline, the step of how many and the whole job's share above the steps; done with the folder path, not saved, failed, gone), `Paused` with "Open Rescribe" (`focus-app`) when the record says `transcribing` or `recorded` and no app tab answers. Polled until the record is gone, so a later Save now or delete shows too. Closing it stops nothing. Writes nothing |
| Storage worker | `workers/storage.ts` | per recording | appends video chunks and PCM blocks to OPFS through one sync access handle per file, flushing after every write; one queue keeps arrival order |
| Transcribe worker | `workers/transcribe.ts` | spawned on the first job, lives as long as the app tab or until a transcribe failure | VAD, then parakeet.js on WASM (default) or WebGPU; one channel per job, jobs serialised, the model kept loaded across jobs and reloaded when a job names another model or backend or after a failure |

Messages that cross `chrome.runtime` (service worker, app tab, content scripts, the dialogs, the processing tab) are typed in `src/lib/messages.ts`; the union there is the single roster of those message types. A worker's `postMessage` protocol is typed in the worker's own file and imported by its one client, so adding a worker touches no shared file.

## Data model (`src/lib/types.ts`)

- `ChannelId`: `'mic' | 'display'`. Two channels, always. A recording may have only one of them.
- `Source`: what a channel captured (`microphone`, `tab`, `window`, `screen`) plus a label (tab title, device name).
- `Recording`: id (UUID), `startedAt`/`endedAt` (epoch ms), title, sources, status (`recording → stopped → recorded → transcribing → done | failed`; `stopped` is the media complete on disk and nothing exported, waiting for the human's decision; `done` and `failed` re-run through `transcribing`), container, `exportRoot` (the name of the recording's own folder after Save as…, absent when it uses the Settings folder), export path (relative to that folder, set once the video is in it), `error` (why it failed; or, when it did not fail, why it is not fully exported: a `recorded` recording without an export path, a `done` one whose transcript stayed in OPFS), `note` (non-failure information: recovered after an interruption, a Meet adapter diagnostic, padded render gaps), `levels`, and at `done` the `model`, `backend` and `transcribedAt` (epoch ms) that produced the transcript.
- `Segment`: `{ channel, start, end, text, words? }`, seconds from recording start. Output of the ASR engine, per channel.
- `SpeakerInterval`: `{ channel, speaker, start, end, source }` with `source` one of `platform | model | channel`. The unit of speaker evidence. Adapters, the diarizer and channel identity all produce these.
- `LabelledSegment`: a `Segment` plus `speaker` and the `evidence` that decided it.
- `Turn`: consecutive labelled segments by the same speaker, merged for rendering.
- `Settings`: path template, my name, container (`webm` | `mp4`), frame rate (5 | 10 | 30), mic default, mic device (`micDeviceId`, a `MediaDeviceInfo.deviceId`; absent: the default device; chosen in the start dialog), model choice, backend (`wasm` | `webgpu`), auto-transcribe (run the pipeline as soon as a recording stops; on by default), and `settingsVersion`, the version the stored object was written at (`SETTINGS_VERSION`; `lib/settings.ts` `migrateSettings` brings an older object up when it is loaded, and the next save writes it back).

## Clock

Recording-relative seconds everywhere in `lib/`. `startedAt` is the epoch ms of the first captured sample, defined precisely as: `Date.now()` taken in the app tab in the same task that creates both AudioWorklet taps (`Recorder.start`, after the AudioContexts are running); `performance.now()` is taken in the same instant as its monotonic twin. Both taps begin at the next render quantum, within ~10 ms. From then on each tap writes exactly one sample per 1/16000 s of *rendered* context time, zeros when its input is absent, so `sample_index / 16000` in either PCM file is recording-relative seconds from `startedAt`, with two qualifications:

- **Render gaps are zero-padded.** When the render thread does not run (an output-device change restarts the sink, a suspended context), no samples are produced and the files would fall behind the clock. The recorder compares each block's position with the monotonic time since start (`recorder/gap.ts`); a lag over 250 ms that persists for a whole second (a main-thread stall drains back to zero within its burst and never pads) is written as that many zero samples into **both** channel files before the block, and `events.jsonl` gets `{ type: 'clock-gap', at, position, paddedSamples }`. The audio between the gap and its detection (up to one second) stays early by the gap. The recording's note counts the gaps.
- **Drift is not corrected.** The audio clock and `Date.now()` disagree by their crystals' ppm; over hours that reaches the width of the attribution tolerance. [[DEFERRED]] § "Anchor platform stamps to the audio clock" is the fix.

The MediaRecorder starts in the same task; its video may begin a frame later, which is playback, not pipeline timing. Content scripts stamp with `Date.now()`; the app tab keeps the stamps as received (`events.jsonl`, written by `Recorder.appendEvent`: `app.ts` forwards every `speaker`, `roster` and `adapter-diagnostic` message that arrives while recording, and on the toolbar path only those from the recorded tab) and converts when it builds evidence (`eventsToEvidence` in `lib/evidence.ts`, through `lib/clock.ts`, called by `assembleTranscript`), so evidence can be rebuilt from disk. See [[INVARIANTS]].

## Storage

- **OPFS**, per recording directory `recordings/<id>/`: `video.mp4` or `video.webm` (MediaRecorder chunks appended), `mic.pcm` and `display.pcm` (16 kHz mono Int16 little-endian, raw, no header; each present only when that channel had an audio track; appended by the AudioWorklet taps via the storage worker), `events.jsonl` (created empty at start; `Recorder.appendEvent` appends one JSON line per adapter message received while recording, as received, and per padded render gap; `parseEventLog` in `lib/assemble.ts` reads it back and skips a malformed line). These four are opened, truncated and written only by the storage worker during the recording. After it, the pipeline writes the **artifacts** through `createWritable` from the app tab (`store.ts` `writeArtifact`): `transcript.md`, `transcript.json`, `meta.json` and `segments.<channel>.json` (the recogniser's output for one channel with the model, backend and Rescribe version that produced it; a re-run skips a channel whose file matches, so a failure on the other channel, a new "my name" or deleted media does not transcribe it again). **Exportable artifact** (`opfs.ts` `isExportableArtifact`): any file in the directory that is not media (`isMediaFile`: the video, the PCM), not `events.jsonl`, not `segments.*.json`, not a `*.crswap` swap file. **Delete media** (`deleteMedia`) removes the video and the PCM only and sets `Recording.mediaDeleted`; the event log, the artifacts and the segments cache stay, so the transcript can still be exported and, from the cache, assembled again (a re-run with another "my name"); without the cache a re-run fails. `deleteRecording` removes the directory and the record.
- **IndexedDB** `rescribe`: `recordings` (the `Recording` records, keyed by id), `handles` (`storage/handles.ts`: the Settings export folder under `export`; a recording's own folder, from Save as…, under `export:<id>`, deleted with the recording).
- **chrome.storage.local**: `Settings` minus the handle.
- **Export folder** (user's disk; `rootFor(rec)`: the recording's own folder when `exportRoot` is set, else the Settings folder): `<template>/recording.mp4|webm` written when the human saves the recording, streamed from the OPFS file (a WebM with its `Duration` written into the header on the way, `export.ts` `exportVideoData`); the exportable artifacts when transcription completes, copied from OPFS (`export.ts` `exportArtifacts`) into the recording's directory as it is: `findExportDirectory` resolves `exportPath` under the current export folder without creating anything and requires the exported video in it, otherwise `ExportError` `missing`. `<template>` comes from `lib/pathtemplate.ts` (`/` nests directories) and the last segment gets ` (2)`, ` (3)`… when it already exists. `Recording.exportPath` is that relative path. When the video export fails (the folder's permission not granted, which is the state after a browser restart or when `finalize` ran outside a click: a recovery on app start; no folder chosen, which only a recovery can meet, since every Save picks one first) the recording stays `recorded` with `exportPath` unset and `error` set; transcription still runs and the artifacts wait in OPFS: the recording ends `done` and `error` still says why ("Not exported: …", never `done` without an export path and without that reason). When only the artifacts' export fails, the recording is `done` with `error` "Transcript not exported: …"; when its directory is no longer under the current folder (the human chose another folder, moved or deleted it), `done` with "Not exported: the recording's folder was not found under the current export folder". Either way the row shows **Save now** (no export path yet, or an export error on a recording that did not fail), and a row with everything in the folder **Re-export**: the same action (`app/library.ts` `reExport`), which works from the stored record, never while the pipeline owns the recording, re-requests the permission from the click, runs `finalize` (the video, unless it is already in its directory; it keeps a `done` recording `done` and a `failed` one `failed`) and then `exportArtifacts`, and clears `error` only when both succeeded.
- **Storage client** (`storage/store.ts`, `storage/export.ts`), what transcription and the library call: `listRecordings`, `getRecording`, `putRecording`, `updateRecording(id, patch)` (a read-modify-write inside one IndexedDB readwrite transaction, `idb.update`, so the recorder's stop, the pipeline's status changes and a rename from the library can interleave without one overwriting another's fields), `getVideoFile(id, container)`, `getChannelFile(id, channel)` (a `File` to slice), `readChannelPcm(id, channel)` (the whole channel as `Int16Array`) + `pcmToFloat32`, `writeArtifact(id, name, text)`, `readArtifact(id, name)`, `listArtifacts(id)` (the exportable artifacts present), `readEventLog(id)`, `segmentsArtifactName(channel)`, `deleteMedia(id)` (video and PCM only; `events.jsonl`, the artifacts, the segments cache and the record stay, `mediaDeleted` is set, the bytes freed are returned), `deleteRecording(id)` (the OPFS directory and the record; the exported files are never touched), `storageUsage()` (one walk of `recordings/`: total, per recording, and the file inventory the library renders from; not `navigator.storage.estimate()`, which counts the cached model too); `getExportRoot`/`setExportRoot`, `getRecordingExportRoot`/`setRecordingExportRoot`/`deleteRecordingExportRoot` (the `export:<id>` handle), `rootFor(rec)`, `ensureExportPermission(rec?)` (call inside a user gesture; the recording's own folder when given one that has it), `exportedFileState(rec)` (`findExportDirectory` as a question: `present`, `missing`, `no-folder`, `permission`; what "Delete media" asks before it removes the local copy), `getExportDirectory(rec, template)` (creates or reopens `<template>/`), `findExportDirectory(rec)` (the existing directory with the video, or `missing`), `writeExportFile(dir, name, data)` (Blob, bytes, string or stream), `exportArtifacts(rec)` (every exportable artifact in OPFS into that directory), `exportVideoName(container)`.

## Pipeline

All five steps are built. Steps 1–2 live in `recorder/recorder.ts`; 3–5 in `app/pipeline.ts` (`transcribeRecording(id)`, `onPipelineProgress`, `isPipelineBusy`, `isTranscribing(id)`), which runs one recording at a time and each channel on its own (the worker restarts after a transcribe failure and rejects whatever is queued behind it), and starts by itself when a saved recording reaches `recorded`, exported or not (Settings "Transcribe as soon as a recording stops", on by default; a `stopped` recording is not transcribed until the human saves it), or from the library. `transcribeRecording` marks the record `transcribing` **when it is queued**, not when its turn comes, so the stored status is what is happening and a library action keyed on it never touches a queued recording; it emits `queued` then, `done` after the final write, and the phases in between. `app.ts` renders the list again on every phase change and exposes `window.rescribe.transcribeRecording(id)` for DevTools and the e2e harness.

1. Capture: streams → two `AudioContext`s (`recorder/graph.ts`): the 16 kHz tap context with one `AudioWorkletNode` tap per channel → 200 ms Int16 blocks → storage worker; the mix context with every channel → `MediaStreamAudioDestinationNode` (mono) → `MediaRecorder` (1 s timeslice; a keyframe every 10 s for WebM, per timeslice for MP4) with the video track (captured at the Settings frame rate, `recorder/capture.ts`) → storage worker. Tab capture also plays the display channel through the mix context's speakers, since Chrome mutes a captured tab for the human.
2. Stop: MediaRecorder stop → last chunk written → taps flushed → storage worker closes the files → `endedAt` → `stopped`. Then the decision (`app/decide.ts`, from the stop dialog, the app tab's Stop, which is Save, or the row): save or save-as → `finalize`: export the video into `<template>/` under `rootFor(rec)`, mark `recorded`; discard → the record, its files and its handle are deleted. A record found at `recording` on app start (tab closed, browser crashed) is finalized from what reached disk (`recoverInterrupted`), `endedAt` from the PCM length, or marked `failed` when no video reached disk; one found at `stopped` keeps waiting (`refreshPending`).
3. Transcribe: per channel present in OPFS, `readChannelPcm` → the transcribe worker (VAD → voiced regions → Parakeet) → `Segment[]`, saved as `segments.<channel>.json`. Progress is a step plan (`lib/progress.ts` `PipelineProgress`: the job's steps in order, made before the first one so the count the human sees never changes: the speech model when the worker may not hold it (`loadedKey`; a model step the worker turns out to need without being planned is added on its first report), each channel with audio as the human's microphone (Settings "my name") or the call, a cached channel already done, who said what, the transcript files when there is an export path; the running step's share, the whole job's share weighted by the steps' usual cost, a rough time left from the job's own pace, never before a channel has been recognised for a while), driven by the worker's reports (bytes per model file, one bar over all of them; per region its position in the recording and the voiced seconds done and in total), and reaches `onPipelineProgress` listeners, the Record section's status line while nothing is being recorded (`summaryLine`), the row and the processing tab (`ui/steps.ts`) through `query-progress`, and the start dialog's one-liner; never the badge, and never a channel id or a region count. A failure marks the recording `failed` with the channel and the stage; a re-run reuses the channels that succeeded. No channel file and no cached channel: `done` with an empty transcript when the video is there (a video-only recording), `failed` when it is not (the media was deleted), the earlier failure's reason kept in front.
4. Attribute: the record is read again first (its title, export path and notes may have moved while the channels ran). `assembleTranscript` (`lib/assemble.ts`, pure): `eventsToEvidence` over the speaker and roster lines of `events.jsonl` with `selfLabel` = Settings "my name" and `micSpeech` = the mic channel's segments (the tile whose speaking coincides with them is the human: its intervals are dropped, it is listed under "my name", and `meta.json` records it as `selfTile`; presentation tiles, a participant's name plus a parenthesised suffix, are not participants), plus channel identity spanning the recording (mic = "my name", display = the one remote participant when the roster has exactly one, else "Speaker") → `attribute()` → `LabelledSegment[]`.
5. Render: `toTurns()` → `transcript.md`; `transcript.json` (turns, labelled segments with words, the model, the backend, the evidence intervals); `meta.json` (`{ version, id, title, startedAt, endedAt, sources, participants, selfTile?, model, backend, container, exportPath, levels, clockGaps, transcribedAt, rescribeVersion }`). Written to OPFS first, then exported into the video's directory (§ Storage: `exportArtifacts`, never a directory without the video); mark `done`, with `error` when anything is not in the folder.

A recording found at `transcribing` on app start was interrupted (reload, crash, while queued or running): `recoverInterruptedTranscriptions` puts it back to `recorded` with a note and the app runs it again once, whatever the auto-transcribe setting; a recording recovered from `recording` follows the setting. Each stage is a function over data on disk, so any stage can be re-run from the library: `transcribeRecording` on a `done` or `failed` recording runs 3–5 again with the cached channels; a `failed` recording's error moves to its note ("Earlier failure: …") so the reason survives the re-run.
