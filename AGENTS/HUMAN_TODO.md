### Choose `~/.rescribe/recordings` as Rescribe's folder

Routines read recordings only from there. In the Rescribe tab: Settings → Choose folder…, `⌘⇧G` in the picker, type `~/.rescribe/recordings`, Select. Until then the stop window offers Index in Lawcel disabled, with that reason under it. Recordings saved before into lawcel-knowledge/meetings/transcripts can run the routine from their rows if their folders are moved into `~/.rescribe/recordings` (the rows find them by the same relative path).
