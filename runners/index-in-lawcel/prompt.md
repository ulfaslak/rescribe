1. **Choose the name** of the meeting's files. Its names are each participant's first name, in the order the existing files in `meetings/transcripts/` use: kenneth, then ulf, then everyone else. Leave out a "participant" that is not a person (a Meet code, "Speaker"). If `meetings/transcripts/<name>.md` already exists for a different meeting, add `-2` (`-3`, …). If it is this same meeting, indexed before, keep the name and update the existing notes rather than write a second set.
2. **Take notes from it**, as you would if asked "take notes from meetings/transcripts/<name>.md". Read the whole transcript and what the repository already knows about the people and topics in it. Write `meetings/notes/<name>.md` following `meetings/notes/_template.md`, with the transcript linked as `[[meetings/transcripts/<name>]]`. Update `crm/`, `decisions/`, `questions/` and `reminders.md` as CLAUDE.md describes. A person the meeting introduces gets a CRM entry.
3. **Put the call's action items on the board**, as CLAUDE.md § Project board and § Reminders describe: what the founders should see when they look at the board becomes a card, what should stay out of view until something happens goes to `reminders.md`, and nothing lives in both. A card the board already has is not filed again: check `.rescribe/board.md`, open and closed, including cards filed from this same meeting indexed before (`filed from the call <name>`). If the call moved an existing card on, say so in the note instead. Most calls give a few cards or none. A card's description is the very short one CLAUDE.md asks for. Assign a card when the call settled who does it: here these instructions override CLAUDE.md's "No assignee by default", and an owner named on the call is assigned. When the call left the owner open, assign no one; the Monday review sets it.
